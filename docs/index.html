Antigravity for Obsidian

Antidian brings Google’s Antigravity agent into your vault, powered by the official Antigravity CLI. Chat with your notes and let the agent read, write and edit them with you. Your folders, tags and naming stay as they are.

How it works

Antidian runs agy in the background and talks to it over the Agent Client Protocol (ACP).

From agy

  • The Antigravity agent and its models
  • Conversations
  • Slash commands and skills

From Antidian

  • An MCP server with tools to search, read and edit your vault through Obsidian
  • The chat sidebar
  • Edit review and undo

Checked before it lands

In every mode, an edit has to target a path inside your vault, a note that hasn’t changed since it was read, and text that matches exactly one place. Otherwise it isn’t applied.

Undo, one edit at a time

Undo puts a note back to the moment before that edit, so later changes to the same note can be lost. Undo history clears when Obsidian closes.

What leaves your computer

Answers come from Google’s Antigravity backend, through its official CLI. Antidian never uploads your whole vault.

Sent to Google

  • The messages you write
  • Images, video and audio you attach
  • The outline of your open note, unless you turn it off
  • Note names, search results and text read during that conversation

Stays on your computer

  • The text of every note a conversation doesn’t read
  • Undo snapshots
  • Titles and pins you give your conversations

If agy isn’t installed, Antidian downloads the official binary and verifies its checksum before running it. You can point to your own copy in Settings → Antidian → Advanced.

Install

You need Obsidian Desktop 1.7.2 or newer and a Google account.

  1. Add the plugin

    This opens Antidian’s page inside Obsidian. Choose Install, then Enable.

    Install in Obsidian

  2. Sign in

    In Settings → Antidian, choose Sign in with Google. The backend downloads on first use.

  3. Ask about a note

    Open the sidebar from the ribbon icon, or run Antidian: Open sidebar.

Nothing opened? Start Obsidian, then search for Antidian in Settings → Community plugins → Browse. To build it yourself, follow the development guide.

Antidian is an independent, open-source plugin. It isn’t made by or affiliated with Obsidian or Google.
